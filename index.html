Privacy Policy

Your security, command transparency & data sovereignty — Crab-bot protects your privacy across Telegram, Discord, WhatsApp, Slack, Signal, Google Chat & iMessage.

Last updated: March 24, 2026 | Version 2.0

Crab-bot Privacy Commitment

Privacy-first philosophy: Crab-bot is a cybersecurity command executor. We collect only essential data to enable command execution, audit trails, and platform security. No message content is sold, mined, or exposed to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with Crab-bot via any integrated messaging app, we process minimal operational data:

User ID (platform) Command history (encrypted, 30d retention) Timestamps & session tokens Execution logs (non-content)
  • Account Identifiers: Unique user IDs from Telegram/Discord/WhatsApp etc. – used to authenticate and route commands.
  • Command Payloads: The exact commands you fire (e.g., nmap -sV target or ./custom-script) are temporarily logged for audit and troubleshooting. Content of messages (non-command) is never stored.
  • Metadata: Platform type, timestamp, response success/failure codes.
  • No sensitive content: We do not collect message text outside command prefixes, nor media files unless explicitly uploaded via command arguments (which are processed ephemerally).

2. How We Use Your Information

Data collected powers the core functionality of Crab-bot and improves security posture:

  • ✅ Execute commands across your favorite chat apps with proper authorization.
  • ✅ Maintain audit trails for forensic analysis & compliance (red team operations).
  • ✅ Detect abuse, prevent unauthorized access, and ensure service stability.
  • ✅ Aggregate anonymized metrics (e.g., number of commands per platform) to enhance performance.

We never use your data for advertising, profiling, or any unrelated marketing activities.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Crab-bot does NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Limited sharing occurs only in these scenarios:

  • Legal compliance: If required by law enforcement or court order (we evaluate each request).
  • Service providers: Cloud infrastructure (encrypted databases) & logging services – all bound by strict NDA and data processing agreements.
  • Security emergencies: To address vulnerabilities, abuse, or imminent threats to infrastructure.

Integrated platforms (Telegram, Discord, etc.) have their own privacy policies; we encourage you to review them. Crab-bot merely acts as a bridge for command execution and does not transfer personal data to them beyond the user ID required for messaging APIs.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain command execution logs for 30 days for operational security and troubleshooting. After this period, logs are automatically purged from our active systems. You can request earlier deletion:

Your control: Send /delete_my_data via any connected chat app or email privacy@crab-bot.io. Your command history, user mapping, and session data will be erased within 72 hours (excluding anonymized aggregates).

Self-hosted instances: Data management is entirely under your control. For cloud-hosted Crab-bot, retention is as described.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location Mzimba, Boma you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Obtain a copy of your personal data held by Crab-bot.
  • Right to Deletion: Request permanent removal of your data.
  • Right to Rectification: Correct any inaccurate information.
  • Right to Opt-out: Disable command execution & bot access anytime by removing the bot from your chat app.

To exercise rights, contact our Data Protection Officer via accuratecyberdefense@tutamail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security:

  • 🔐 Encryption: All command logs & user identifiers stored in AES-256 encrypted databases.
  • 🛡️ API authentication: Unique tokens per platform, MFA where supported.
  • 📡 Audit trails: Every command is associated with a tamper-proof hash.
  • 🌐 Network isolation: Crab-bot runs in isolated containers with strict egress controls.

Despite best efforts, no system is 100% immune. We conduct regular penetration tests and bug bounties.

7. Children's Privacy

Crab-bot is not intended for individuals under 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we become aware of such data, we will delete it immediately.

8. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

The Crab-bot website uses essential cookies for authentication and preference storage. No third-party tracking or analytics cookies are deployed without consent. You may disable cookies via browser settings, but some site features may be affected. The bot itself does not use browser cookies.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect operational changes or legal requirements. Material changes will be announced via our community channels and the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of Crab-bot after modifications constitutes acceptance.
